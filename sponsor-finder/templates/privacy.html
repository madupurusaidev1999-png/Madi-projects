UK Visa Sponsor Finder

Privacy

Last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

We don't ask for your name or email, we don't use cookies, and we don't track you with analytics or advertising.

Your searches

When you search, your browser downloads the sponsor register once and searches it on your own device. The words you type are not sent to us.

Services we rely on

  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and keeps them for security. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads. See the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.
  • Links to Companies House, GOV.UK and Google open those websites, which have their own privacy policies.

If you email us

If you write to hello@ukvisasponsorfinder.com, we use your email address only to reply, and delete the conversation when it's no longer needed.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask what information we hold about you and ask us to delete it. Email us at the address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

About the data on this site

Company information comes from the Home Office register of licensed sponsors and from Companies House, both published under the Open Government Licence v3.0. If information about your organisation is wrong, the source register needs correcting. Contact us and we'll point you to the right place.

UK Visa Sponsor Finder is independent and not affiliated with the Home Office, UK Visas and Immigration or Companies House. Back to search
