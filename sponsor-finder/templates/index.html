About this data. Every company here comes from the Home Office Register of licensed sponsors: workers, which is updated every working day. The register lists only the name, location, licence type, rating and visa route. “First seen” is the date this site first found the company on the register, not the official licence date. “Removed” means the company has dropped off the register since we started tracking. The Home Office does not publish licence grant dates or when a company last sponsored someone. An A rating means the licence is in good standing. A B rating means the Home Office has downgraded it and the company cannot issue new certificates of sponsorship until it is fixed.
