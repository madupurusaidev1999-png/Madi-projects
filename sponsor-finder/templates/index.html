Reading a result

Licensed

The organisation holds an A-rated licence. It can assign certificates of sponsorship, the document you need to apply for a work visa.

B-rated

The Home Office has downgraded the licence, usually after a compliance problem. The employer can't assign new certificates until it is back to an A rating.

Not listed

No licence under that name. Search a shorter part of the name, since the register uses the legal company name, which can differ from the brand you know.

Does a licence mean they will sponsor me?

No. It means they are allowed to. Whether they sponsor depends on the role. The job must also meet the visa rules, including an eligible occupation and the minimum salary. Ask the employer directly.

Where does the data come from?

The Home Office Register of licensed sponsors: workers, published every working day, and the free Companies House company data, published monthly.

What does the incorporation date mean?

It is when the company was set up at Companies House, not when it got its sponsor licence. The Home Office doesn't publish licence dates or how many workers a company has sponsored.

How accurate are the company details?

Companies House records are matched by company name, which works for about 4 in 5 sponsors. Charities, NHS bodies, sole traders and businesses trading under another name often have no match. Open the Companies House record to confirm.
